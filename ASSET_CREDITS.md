## Sentinel player and synthesized soundscapes (v0.5.0)

`assets/models/sentinel.glb` is an original armored player mesh with its own original skeleton and Run/Idle/Jump animations, generated by `art_sources/build_sentinel.py`. It does not reuse Sinbad geometry or animation. The player, team outline shader, and material work use the project's **MIT** license.

The six Ogg files in `assets/audio/` are original **MIT**-licensed synthesis: Brown Ambience from `art_sources/build_soundscapes.py`; the five biome music files (Verdant Circuit, Cold Reliquary, Ember Pressure, Root Signal and Tidal Relay) were replaced in v0.8.0 with arena-techno synthesis from `art_sources/build_arena_audio.py`. No recordings, commercial tracks, third-party samples, or copied melodies are used. Software used to generate them: NumPy, SciPy and ffmpeg; their licenses do not replace the project's license for these generated audio files.

Minion variants continue to use the adapted Sinbad creature by Zi Ye under **CC BY-SA 3.0**, with additional tinting and original treasure ornaments. Gold soul particles, the redesigned soul glyph, circular controls, and reverse icon are original MIT project content.


## Original reload foley (v0.7.0)

`assets/audio/reload_lower.wav` and `reload_raise.wav` are original **MIT**-licensed mechanical sound effects synthesized by `art_sources/build_reload_sounds.py` using filtered noise and damped oscillators. No recordings or samples from GoldenEye or any other game are used. The lower/raise reload motion is original project code inspired by the requested classic presentation style.

## Pump shotgun, anatomical hands and arena audio (v0.8.0)

`assets/models/pump_shotgun.glb` and `human_harvest.glb` are original MIT-licensed models generated by `art_sources/build_pump_viewmodel.py`, with separate pump and anatomical arm/hand parts. No reference-game geometry is used.

`shotgun_shot.wav`, `shotgun_pump.wav`, `shotgun_load.wav` and `shotgun_ready.wav` are original MIT-licensed synthesized effects from `art_sources/build_arena_audio.py`, which also generates the five replacement arena-techno tracks. No third-party samples or recordings are used. Circular map geometry, projectile visuals, weather effects and touch joystick are original MIT project content.

## Original viewmodel textures and refinements (v0.9.0)

`assets/textures/viewmodel/` contains original tileable PBR color, normal and roughness maps for skin, machined steel, cloth and walnut, generated by `art_sources/build_viewmodel_textures.py` (NumPy, SciPy, Pillow). They are also embedded into the shotgun/arm and harvesting-hand GLBs by the updated `build_pump_viewmodel.py`. The maps, smoother hand geometry and extra shotgun details are original **MIT** project content. These are synthesized materials, not third-party scans or photographs. Arched gatehouse geometry, lock rings/brackets and the touch hold control are original MIT project content.

## Plasma Armory (v0.11.0)

`assets/models/double_barrel.glb` and `plasma_scythe.glb` are original MIT-licensed models built by `art_sources/build_armory.py`, reusing the project's original PBR steel/walnut materials. The double barrel, forged crescent/plasma edge, staff, team-color handling and radial weapon-wheel graphics are original project content. Generated embedded PNGs beside these GLBs are copies of the original project maps and use the same MIT license.

`assets/audio/scythe_swing.wav` is original MIT-licensed synthesis using filtered noise, a swept oscillator and an amplitude envelope; no external recordings/samples are used. Its reproducible builder is `art_sources/build_scythe_audio.py`.

## Boroughs and Careers (v0.12.0)

The Carbon Carbine and Warden Sniper GLBs and extracted material PNGs are original MIT project content, built by `art_sources/build_military_rifles.py` using the original steel PBR maps. Their new shot effects are original sample-free synthesis from `art_sources/build_military_audio.py`.

The procedural Art Deco city sky, stepped skyline buildings, structural foundations, vines and foliage, directional damage UI, hero emblems, names and career chapter text are original MIT project content. The 24 profiles currently share the existing Sentinel combat model; the roster is not a collection of 24 finished character meshes.

## Nightfall sky and soft lens (v0.13.0)

The procedural stars, small moon, 3D-density sky clouds and screen/depth lens shader are original MIT project code. No photographic sky, moon textures or third-party post-processing packages were added. Moon surface variation and clouds are synthesized in the shader. The lens/settings UI use the same original project code and existing OFL font.
